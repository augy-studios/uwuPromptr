# Google Play release notes, 2.0.0

Paste the block below into the release's **Release notes** box in Play Console (GOOGLE-PLAY.md,
section 10). Play allows 500 characters per language; this is about 450. The tag must match a
language the store listing has: the app is in English only, so the listing's default language is
English (United Kingdom), `en-GB`. If Play shows the tag as one it doesn't know, change it to the
one Play lists.

This is uwuPromptr's first release on Google Play, so the notes say what the app is rather than
what changed.

```text
<en-GB>
uwuPromptr, the teleprompter that works offline, now on Google Play.
• Write a script, press play, and it scrolls at the speed you set
• Speed and font size a step at a time while it runs; mirror it for glass
• Your phone as the remote by a six-letter code, or phone to phone without wifi
• A Wear OS watch on your wrist as a remote too
• Material You by default, with the UwU Apps look a switch away
• Your scripts stay on your phone: no account, no ads
</en-GB>
```

Play shows its own update notices, so the Google Play build has no update bar of its own; its
Updates page lists every version and sends you to Play to update.
